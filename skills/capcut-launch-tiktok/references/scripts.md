## 档位一：12 秒 · 钩子型（首投测试用）

砍掉失败分支。6–7 句。适合 A/B 测开头。

```markdown
| 时间码 | 字幕 | 词数 | 动词 | 段 | 画面 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.4 | *（无字幕，结果先行）* | 0 | — | ① | 角色特写 / 效果 |
| 1.4–2.8 | pick a look | 3 | pick | ② | 效果对比 |
| 2.8–4.2 | it's 2 taps | 3 | — | ② | 界面 |
| 4.2–5.6 | first, this arrow | 3 | — | ③ | 箭头 / 分享键 |
| 5.6–7.6 | try template — bottom left | 4 | — | ③ | 左下角（CTA 在此句） |
| 7.6–9.4 | hit use template, pick a photo | 5 | hit / pick | ③ | 模板页 → 相册 |
| 9.4–11.4 | tap next | 2 | tap | ③ | 下一步 |
| 11.4–12.6 | and you're in | 3 | — | ④ | 生成结果 |
```

统计：8 句 / 23 词 / 平均 2.9。
**省略声明**：砍掉「if it's not there」失败分支。理由：锚点存在时走不到，首投优先保证节奏。

## 可复用的句库

**降门槛（②）**
- `it's 2 taps`
- `it's actually super easy`
- `let me teach you how`
- `takes like a minute`
- `no editing skills needed`

**定位（③，必须有至少一句）**
- `in the bottom left corner`
- `next to the search bar`
- `top right of the screen`
- `on the red button`

**失败分支（③）**
- `if it's not there`
- `if you don't see it`
- `no template? just`

**CTA 藏点（③，不要把 download/install 写出来）**
- `try template — bottom left`
- `hit use template`
- `pick a look, that's it`

**收尾（④，≤4 词）**
- `and you're done`
- `and you're in`
- `that's the whole thing`
